The program

Chamber music

Weekly coaching in small groups with University of Ottawa faculty, working toward a performance at the end of each term and at the Kiwanis Music Festival.

Masterclasses

Sixteen a year with guest artists from the University and the region: eight for violin, four for viola, and four for cello.

Performance class

Regular chances to play for the other students in the program and to give each other feedback.
