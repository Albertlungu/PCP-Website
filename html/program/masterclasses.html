Masterclasses

Guest artists from the University of Ottawa and the surrounding region give sixteen masterclasses a year: eight for violin, four for viola, and four for cello.

A few students play for the guest in each class, and everyone else listens and learns from the feedback. To play in an upcoming masterclass, sign up to perform.
