About the program

The Pre-College Program is for high school students in the Ottawa–Gatineau area who play at an advanced level, many of them thinking about studying music after high school. Students need to be at RCM Grade 9 or above.

The program adds to a student's private lessons; it does not replace them. Students work with University of Ottawa faculty and teachers from across the region, alongside students from many different studios.

What happens on a Saturday

Chamber groups meet with their coaches every week. Most Saturdays also have a masterclass with a guest artist or a performance class, where students play for each other. The calendar lists who is playing each week.

Expectations

Students are expected to come prepared and on time, and to follow the code of conduct.
