Code of conduct

Every student in the program agrees to the following. Most of it comes down to being prepared and treating the people around you well.

  1. Respect the people around you

    Be courteous to other students, coaches, staff, and guest artists. Listen properly when others are playing or speaking, and don’t disrupt rehearsals, classes, or performances.

  2. Come prepared

    Arrive on time and ready for every rehearsal, coaching, and performance, and practise between Saturdays so your group can make progress.

  3. Work with your group

    Take part actively in rehearsals. If something isn’t working, talk to your group or your coach directly and constructively.

  4. Look after the building

    Treat the rooms, instruments, and equipment of the university with care, and follow the program’s rules, including those on safety and security.

  5. Represent the program well

    When you perform or appear in public as part of the program, you represent it. Behave honestly and professionally.

  6. Make room for everyone

    Students and teachers come from many backgrounds and studios. Everyone should feel welcome.

  7. Help the program improve

    Tell your coaches and the program’s organizers what is working and what isn’t.

If the code is broken

Violations of this code of conduct may result in disciplinary action, including verbal warnings, probation, suspension, or dismissal from the program, depending on the severity and frequency of the offense. No refunds will be issued if a student is dismissed as a result of disciplinary action.
